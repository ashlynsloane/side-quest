# V18.11.4 — Palette Differentiation

V18.11.4 corrects two problems revealed by on-device review:

1. Several swim, mountain, and aero palettes looked like minor teal-blue
   variations.
2. Warm-paper surfaces became yellow backgrounds behind nearly every block of
   text.

The revised system keeps reading surfaces neutral. Sport character now comes
from the page canvas, primary controls, borders, muted support areas, and
accent colors.

- Trail Club: pine, trail-map gray, weathered blue, and rust
- Summit Run: granite violet, ice blue, and summit orange
- Race Day: navy, sharp white, timing gray, and signal orange
- Open Water: cobalt, turquoise, bright white, and coral
- Alpine Lake: eucalyptus green, dock gray, and quiet lake blue
- Road Club: concrete, burgundy, jersey navy, and red
- Aero Lab: carbon, metallic gray, electric cyan, and magenta
- Singletrack: dusty green, evergreen, clay, and moss

Theme Lab now groups environments under General, Run, Swim, and Bike. Each
theme card uses a neutral surface with its own accent border and independent
swatches.
