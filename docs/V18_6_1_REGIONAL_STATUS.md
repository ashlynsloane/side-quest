# Side Quest V18.6.1 — Regional Status

## Why the regional exposure screen changed

A regional percentage has no universal target. Quadriceps do not need to represent a fixed share of training, and biceps do not need to equal another region. The correct comparison depends on the athlete's current Side Quest plan, training phase, completed strength work, personal endurance history, symptoms, and restrictions.

V18.6.1 replaces the default Regional Exposure display with Regional Status.

## Three separate inputs

### Direct-strength coverage

Each region is compared with the direct-strength exposures actually scheduled for that region in the current training week.

Example: `1 of 2 planned direct-strength exposures completed`.

A region that was not targeted by the plan is labeled `Not targeted this week`; Side Quest does not invent a universal quota.

### Sport-related demand

The most recent seven days of mapped swim, bike, and run demand are compared with the athlete's own preceding 14–30 day baseline, normalized to a weekly equivalent.

Labels are:

- Lower than usual for you
- Typical for you
- Above usual for you
- Much above usual for you
- Not enough personal history for comparison

Sport load remains planning context. It cannot erase a direct-strength gap and never becomes exercise-specific strength credit.

### Recovery and restriction context

Regional status also considers:

- today's readiness check-in;
- muscle freshness;
- active pain/tightness check-ins;
- hard PT exclusions;
- recency of direct strength and sport load.

The app uses cautious wording such as `No current recovery concern detected`, `Recovery uncertain`, `Review before loading`, and `Restricted`. It does not claim that a region is fully recovered.

## Statuses

- **Strength opportunity** — planned direct strength remains and no major current concern is detected.
- **Sport-loaded, strength still needed** — endurance demand is elevated, but planned direct strength remains important.
- **On plan** — the current regional strength target has been met.
- **Recently loaded** — the region has recently received direct or sport-related loading; the planned stimulus may still remain appropriate.
- **Review before loading** — readiness or symptom information warrants review before more high-load work.
- **Restricted** — an active hard restriction applies.
- **Not targeted this week** — the current block does not prescribe direct work for that region.

## Visual model

The anatomy fill shows the strength-planning status. Region buttons receive an amber ring when sport demand is above the athlete's own recent baseline. An exclamation mark identifies a review or restriction signal.

## Guardrails

Regional Status does not:

- diagnose overtraining;
- predict injury;
- prove recovery;
- impose a universal regional percentage;
- replace athlete or clinician judgment.
